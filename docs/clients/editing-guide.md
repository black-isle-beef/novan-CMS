# How to edit your site

A one-page guide for the people who look after a site's words and pictures. It works for every Novan site. Your
agency gives you a link to sign in; keep this page next to you for the first few edits.

## Sign in

Open the link from your invitation email and choose your own password. Next time, go to the same address and sign
in. If you forget your password, choose **Forgotten your password?** on the sign-in page.

## Change a page

1. Choose **Pages**, then the page you want.
2. The page opens in the editor: your site on the right, a panel on the left. Click any text or picture on the site
   to edit it in the panel. What you change shows on the right straight away, but nobody else sees it yet.
3. Choose **Save draft** to keep your changes without showing them, or **Publish changes** when you are happy. The
   live site updates within a minute or two.

## Make a new page

1. Choose **Pages**, then **New page**. Give it a title; the address (like `/opening-times`) is filled in for you.
2. Choose **Create page**, then **Add block** to build it up. A block is one section of a page: a heading banner, a
   piece of text, an image, a row of cards, a button. Each block has its own options (such as colour and layout) in
   the panel; every option is designed to look right and be easy to read, so try them freely.
3. Drag blocks to reorder them, or use the block's menu to duplicate or delete one.
4. Choose **Publish**. To show the page in the menu, open **Navigation** (search for it if you cannot see it) and
   add a link to the page.

## Add a picture

1. In an image block (or any picture on a page), choose the image, then upload a file from your computer or pick
   one already in **Media**.
2. Fill in the **Alt text**: one short sentence saying what the picture shows, as you would describe it on the
   phone, for example "A cockapoo with a freshly trimmed face". People using screen readers hear this instead of
   seeing the picture, and search engines read it too. A page with an image but no alt text cannot be published.
3. Photos straight from a phone are fine: the site makes smaller copies for each screen automatically.

## Undo a mistake

Every save is kept. Open the page, choose **Version history**, pick an earlier version and choose
**Restore this version**. To take a page off the site without deleting it, choose **Unpublish**.

## Things the agency looks after

The site's design and colours, new kinds of blocks, the domain, and anything technical. Ask them for these; you
cannot break the design from the editor.

## Getting help

Write down what you were trying to do, what you expected and what happened instead (a screenshot helps), and send it
to your agency contact. During the first two weeks after launch every question is logged and answered, so ask
anything, however small.
